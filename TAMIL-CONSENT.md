# Tamil consent text, for translation

The review asked for Tamil consent wording on Tamil-targeted advertisements.
Translate the English below and send it back; I will wire it in. I have not
machine-translated it, because a consent statement is the one line on the page
where an approximate translation is a legal problem rather than a style one.

## 1. Consent checkbox, beside the enquiry form

This is the sentence with the tick box. It is what the student is agreeing to,
so it has to be accurate rather than smooth.

> I'd like a LIMRA counsellor to contact me by phone, WhatsApp or email
> about studying MBBS in the Philippines.
>
> I'd like a LIMRA counsellor to contact me by phone, WhatsApp or email
> about studying MBBS in Timor-Leste.
>
> I'd like a LIMRA counsellor to contact me by phone, WhatsApp or email
> about DMSF.

The country or university name is the only part that changes between
pages, so translate one and tell me how the name slots in grammatically.
In Tamil the noun may need a case ending that English does not have.

The review also suggested a shorter version, which is worth translating instead
if you prefer it:

> By submitting this form, I agree to receive admission-related calls and
> WhatsApp messages from LIMRA. Privacy Policy.

## 2. Supporting lines near the form

> No obligation

> Callback during business hours

> No spam, ever.

> Privacy Policy

## Notes for whoever translates

- Keep "LIMRA", "WhatsApp", "NEET" and "FMGE" in English. They are read as
  names, and transliterating them makes them harder to recognise, not easier.
- "Privacy Policy" stays a link, so it needs to work as link text on its own.
- Send the translation as plain text, not as a screenshot or a PDF, so the
  characters come through intact.
